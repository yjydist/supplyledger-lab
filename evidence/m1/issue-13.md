## First-run provenance and trust roles

The current static check establishes that **present files** match the committed certificate inventory. It cannot prove the historical order of first issuance. That order is documented in the [#9 native CA ledger](issue-9.md#native-command-sequence-and-result) and [#10 per-identity native ledger](issue-10.md#native-command-envelope-and-outcomes), with the [CA runbook](../../docs/m1-ca-native.md) and [identity runbook](../../docs/m1-identities-native.md) showing the actual native command shape. All 24 distinct #9 and 58 distinct #10 ignored log paths referenced by those reports were present in the retained `.runtime/ca-logs` during this review. Their redacted result summaries are committed; the private logs themselves were not opened, copied or claimed as a new run.

The #9 sequence began with Seller TLS CA initialization and a failed first service start: `tls.keyfile` pointed at a nonexistent file. The TLS CA template was corrected in place and the same initialized Seller root/DB/volume was retained; a second start and verified HTTPS probe passed. Seller Enrollment CA and the first `seller-operator1` register/enroll followed as separate native calls. #9 then initialized the remaining seven CAs with separate commands. #10 separately enrolled four additional registrar clients and ran 27 distinct registration/enrollment pairs. The historical first-start **FAIL** and successful recovery are both retained in #9; this report does not turn the first attempt into a PASS.
